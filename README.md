# CoreRadio

### Radio from the whole world. In your phone.

**[Download](releases/CoreRadio-1.1.apk)** · Android 9+ · no account · no ads

---

Night. A car. Local radio plays an ad. Another. Then a hit you have known since last winter.

Country. Genre. Language. A station from a city you have never been to. Play.

The music keeps going when you lock the screen. Then a song you do not want to lose. You turn on recording. The app waits for the start. It saves the whole track.

Morning is not a beep. It is the last station.

---

Discover stations from around the world. Save favourites. Add your own if it is missing from the catalogue.

Listen after you close the window. A sleep timer. A full song, not a fragment. A morning alarm that starts the radio.

The last station is waiting. It will not start itself. You press Play.

---

For anyone who wants something other than the three stations in the car. For anyone who would rather wake to music than a beep.

This is not a streaming service with its own catalogue. It does not own the stations. It does not put your music in the cloud.
